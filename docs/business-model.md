# Aged Care Services Manager Business Model

## Value Proposition

An aged care facility administrator can operate independently using:
- Document-handling robots for staff scheduling, resident enrollment, incident logging
- This actor for administrative proposal and governance
- Their own audit ledger instead of a closed SaaS

Results: lower total cost of ownership, full operational control, regulatory transparency.

## Core Activities

### 1. Staffing Schedule Management

- Propose staff rosters for shifts (dietary, nursing, personal care, activities)
- Governor checks facility and staff records
- Robot enacts approved schedule changes

### 2. Resident Record Administration

- Log resident enrollment, updates to non-clinical administrative data
- Governor checks facility and resident registration
- NOT clinical data: care plans, diagnoses, medications remain in clinical systems

### 3. Family Correspondence

- Draft family updates, bills, general communications
- Governor checks facility provenance
- Robot dispatches approved messages

### 4. Incident Logging and Flagging

- Flag safety incidents, quality concerns, regulatory matters
- Governor ALWAYS escalates to human review (no auto-approval)
- Incident record is created only after human sign-off

## Revenue Streams (for operators implementing this)

1. **Base service**: Subscriptions for actor licensing and maintenance
2. **Compliance reporting**: Audit ledger and regulatory dashboards
3. **Robot hardware**: Physical document scanning and communication dispatch
4. **Consultation**: Staff training, governance customization, facility licensing support

## Regulatory Compliance

- All administrative records are logged and auditable
- Clear separation of clinical vs. administrative authority
- Clinical decisions never touched by this actor
- Incident flagging creates human review checkpoint

## Risk Mitigation

- Governor hard blocks on unregistered facilities/residents
- Governor hard blocks on clinical decision language
- All escalations (incident flagging) require explicit human approval
- Append-only ledger prevents tampering with records
